placethedude

I felt that The Dude was sorely missed as a placeholder option, so why not rectify the situation? Add your desired width and height after the URL and a placeholder will be generated.

Example: https://placethedude.com/img/300/200
Image endpoint lives under /img so CloudFront can route static pages and generated images cleanly
